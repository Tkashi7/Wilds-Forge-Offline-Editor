## 🖥️ Interface & Experience

The interface is built around a philosophy we call **"the hunter's journal."** The left column is your roster of hunters; the center canvas is the current edit surface; the right column is a live preview of stats and pouch contents. Menus are keyboard-first with full shortcut coverage, but every command is also reachable through a mouse or touchscreen. Panels dock and undock freely, and a layout-saving feature lets you restore your favorite arrangement with a single keystroke.

**Accessibility touchpoints:**
- Screen-reader friendly labels on all controls.

## 🧩 Modules Breakdown

### ❤️ Vitality Control

The Vitality module adjusts the health pool and stamina ceiling of your selected hunter. You can set a fixed value, allow percentage-based scaling, or simply pin your hunter's health to a stable ceiling for testing purposes. All changes are sandboxed until you commit them.

### ⚔️ Damage Multiplier

A single slider that gently amplifies or dampens the damage your hunter deals. Useful for stress-testing builds, exploring theoretical maximums, and understanding how damage formulas respond to different combinations of skills.

### 🎒 Inventory & Pouch Editor

A full catalog view of every item in the game, categorized by rarity, type, and use case. Drag items into your pouch, adjust stack counts, or lock slots so they cannot be accidentally altered. Search supports fuzzy matching, so even a half-remembered name gets you where you want to go.

### 🏆 Reward Sculptor

Controls what appears at the end of a hunt. Choose which categories of rewards roll into your post-hunt screen, adjust rarity ceilings, and preview the exact reward table before you commit the change. The Sculptor is deterministic — the same inputs always produce the same outputs.

### 🛠️ Weapon & Armor Workshop

Deep customization for gear: choose base templates, adjust sharpness, tweak elemental values, reroll decoration slots, and preview how the final item will look in your equipment box. Sets can be saved as loadouts and swapped in one click.

### 📊 Stats & Profile Analyzer

Visualizes the derived statistics of your current build: effective health, expected damage per combo, elemental breakpoints, and resistances. Graphs animate as you drag sliders in the other modules, so you can see trade-offs as you make them.

## 🔐 Privacy & Safety Philosophy

This toolkit is **offline by design**. There is no cloud synchronization, no analytics ping, no background phone-home, and no account requirement. Your save file is read and written locally; nothing leaves your machine unless you explicitly export a profile to share with a friend.

We also believe in transparency: every module documents exactly which bytes of the save it touches, in plain language, so you can audit what is happening underneath the hood.
